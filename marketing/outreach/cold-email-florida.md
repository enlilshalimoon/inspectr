# Cold email — Florida home inspectors

Same rules, footer, cadence and logging as `cold-email-texas.md`. Leads:
`leads-fl-*.csv` (gitignored). Lookover does full home inspection reports with a
systems inventory (inspected / not inspected / not present) per Florida's SOP. It
does NOT fill out Citizens 4-point or wind-mitigation forms, so never claim it does.

## Email 1 (day 0)

**Subject (A/B test, alternate by lead):**
- A: `{first_name}, quick one about your reports`
- B: `Report done before you leave the driveway?`

Hey {first_name},

I'm Enlil, I built an app for home inspectors called Lookover. {personal_line}

You walk the house and take photos like normal, say a few words about each
defect, and the finding is written for you in SOP language before you leave the
property. Every system gets its inspected / not inspected / not present line, and
you approve every word before anything goes to the client.

The whole point is getting the report out the same afternoon instead of
writing it that night.

Would you try it on one inspection this week? Free, no card, and I'll set it up
with you on a 10 minute call if that helps.

Enlil
Lookover · uselookover.com/fl
