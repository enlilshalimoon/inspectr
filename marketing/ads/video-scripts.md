# Ad video scripts — shootable

> The `campaign-agent-prompt.md` has the concept menu and brand rules. This doc turns
> three of those concepts into scripts you can hand to yourself (iPhone), a friend
> with a decent phone, or a $200 Fiverr editor. Each script is fully specified: shot
> list, exact narration, on-screen text, timing, aspect ratio.
>
> All three follow OFFER-POLICY.md — no "free for life" in cold ads. Founding-price
> scarcity only. All three should be exported at both 1:1 (for FB/IG feed) and 9:16
> (for Stories/Reels).

---

## Script 1 — Founder explainer, 60 seconds

**Category:** Founder-on-camera. Highest-converting format for cold B2B SaaS.
**Where it runs:** Feed placements. Sound-on autoplay assumption.
**Effort to make:** 30 minutes. Just you, a phone, and a well-lit room.
**Why it works:** Inspectors trust people who built things for them. Polished production actually *reduces* trust in this audience. Bad lighting is a feature.

### Production guidance

- Shoot vertical (9:16). Center-frame yourself in the upper 60%, leave dead space at bottom for captions.
- Also do a 1:1 crop from the same footage for feed. Frame accordingly.
- Phone camera. Front-facing is fine. No ring light, no lav mic — sounds too produced.
- Location: your desk, your kitchen, your truck. Anywhere real. Not a studio.
- Wear: work-appropriate. T-shirt, henley, casual button-down. Never a suit.
- Look into the camera lens, not the screen.
- **Do not read.** Rehearse the beats, then talk. Stumbling is fine — it reads as honest.

### Shot list (2 shots total)

**Shot A (55 sec) — Talking head.** You, phone-cam, upper-body framing. Natural light from a window camera-left. Slightly off-center in frame (rule of thirds).

**Shot B (5 sec, cutaway) — Product demo.** Screen-record of your phone using Lookover — one photo capture → voice waveform → AI draft appearing. Overlaid at ~40-45 sec mark while you say "the tool you're seeing on screen…"

### Narration (verbatim — beats, not a script to read)

> Hey — I'm Enlil. I built Lookover because I kept watching residential home inspectors finish an inspection at three o'clock and then spend from eight P.M. to midnight typing the report.
>
> **[pause — half beat]**
>
> The tool you're seeing on screen — you walk the property, you take photos, and you just talk into your phone like you're explaining what you're seeing to the client.
>
> **[cutaway to Shot B for 5 sec — you keep talking over it]**
>
> By the time you sit down at your laptop, the report's drafted in standard InterNACHI SOP language. You review it, edit anything you want, hit approve — thirty minutes instead of three hours.
>
> **[back to Shot A]**
>
> Fourteen-day free trial. No credit card. First fifty inspectors lock in seventy-nine a month, and that price never goes up.
>
> Link in the comments — or just search Lookover. Built by an actual person you can email back.

### On-screen text (captions burned in for sound-off viewing)

- 0-5s: `Enlil — Founder, Lookover`
- Full-caption transcription in white sans-serif at bottom third, matching your speech word-by-word (use CapCut auto-caption then edit).
- 55-60s: End card — solid navy background, white text:
  - `LOOKOVER`
  - `14-day trial · no card · uselookover.com`
  - Orange CTA button graphic: `Start free trial →`

### Post-production

- Total runtime: 55-60 sec (Meta cuts at 60)
- Export: MP4, H.264, 1080×1920 (9:16) and 1080×1080 (1:1)
- File size: under 20 MB per version
- Audio: original (no music). Music makes it feel like an ad.

### Meta ad copy paired with this creative

- **Primary text:** "Built for inspectors who want their evenings back. 30 minutes per report instead of 3 hours of typing. See the whole workflow ↓"
- **Headline:** `Talk through the inspection. Get home earlier.`
- **CTA:** `Sign Up`
- **URL:** `https://www.uselookover.com/signup?utm_source=meta&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=founder-video&utm_content=v1`

## Script 2 — Silent product demo, 30 seconds

**Category:** Demo-only. No people, no talking, no music. Just the product doing the thing.
**Where it runs:** Feed placements. Sound-off assumption (95% of Meta feed watches muted).
**Effort to make:** 2-3 hours of screen recording + editing.
**Why it works:** Shows the actual thing you're selling in 30 seconds. Zero salesmanship = zero skepticism.

### Shot list (5 shots, tight cuts)

**Shot 1 (0-5s) — Phone in hand, water heater photo.**
- Screen recording of the Lookover mobile app.
- User taps the camera icon → photo of a water heater snaps into the finding card.

**Shot 2 (5-11s) — Voice note recording.**
- User taps the mic button, orange waveform animates for 5 seconds while they speak (audio muted in final export, waveform visible).
- Transcript appears in gray at bottom: *"Water heater is a 2009 Rheem 40-gallon. T&P discharge terminates above the pan, needs to be within 6 inches of the floor."*

**Shot 3 (11-18s) — Finding card appears.**
- Cut to: on the same screen, a white card slides in below the waveform with the AI-drafted finding: title, "Moderate" orange pill, body text.
- On-screen text (top): `~15 seconds later`

**Shot 4 (18-25s) — Laptop review interface.**
- Cut to: laptop screen showing the desktop review with 5 findings listed, one being edited with severity dropdown open.
- Cursor arrow visible, clicking "Approve all" button.

**Shot 5 (25-30s) — Public share link view.**
- Cut to: phone screen (different phone, could be a family member's) showing the buyer-facing report — clean header, roof photo, "Flashing at vent pipe · Major" card.
- On-screen text (top): `Delivered to the client. Same day.`

### On-screen text throughout

- 0-5s: `1. Snap. Talk.` (bottom-left, small caps)
- 5-11s: `2. AI drafts the finding.` (same position)
- 11-18s: `In SOP language. Cited to code.` (same)
- 18-25s: `3. Review. Edit. Approve.` (same)
- 25-30s: End card fades in:
  - `LOOKOVER`
  - `30 min · not 3 hours`
  - `14-day trial · no card · $79/mo founding price · first 50`
  - Orange button graphic: `Try it →`

### Sound

None. Silent. If Meta requires audio track, use a barely-audible ambient bed at -35dB.

### Post-production

- Total runtime: exactly 30 sec
- Export: 1080×1920 (9:16) and 1080×1080 (1:1) — for 1:1 you'll need to re-frame the phone shots slightly
- Captions: ALL text must be burned in. This is a silent ad.
- Use CapCut, DaVinci Resolve, or Final Cut. Doesn't need to be fancy.

### Meta ad copy paired with this creative

- **Primary text:** "The whole inspection report — from your walkthrough. Watch how ↓"
- **Headline:** `Capture → Draft → Done.`
- **CTA:** `Learn More`
- **URL:** `https://www.uselookover.com/?utm_source=meta&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=demo-silent&utm_content=v1`

## Script 3 — Static "the math" — 15 sec animated or single image

**Category:** Text-forward, no people, no product demo. Pure hook.
**Where it runs:** Both feed and stories. Baseline "control" ad to test other creatives against.
**Effort to make:** 30 min in Figma or Canva. Or a single Photoshop file.
**Why it works:** Doesn't require watching. The value prop hits in 2 seconds of thumb-scroll.

### Format: single image (fastest to make)

**Canvas:** 1080×1080 (feed) and 1080×1920 (stories).
**Background:** Solid cream `#fef9f3` (matches brand palette).

**Composition:**

Top third:
- Very small `LOOKOVER` wordmark, upper-left corner, navy `#0f172a`
- Large centered text, navy `#0f172a`, bold Inter:
  - Line 1: `3 hours of typing.`
  - Line 2 (strike-through effect on "hours", replaced above): actually just:
  - Show two rows visually:
    - `❌  3 hrs typing` — strikethrough, grey
    - `✅  30 min reviewing` — bold, navy, twice as big

Middle:
- Thin orange horizontal divider, 40% width, centered
- Below divider, medium text, navy: `Photo + voice → AI draft → approve.`
- Sub-copy, smaller, slate-grey: `Standard InterNACHI SOP language. Your license, your sign-off.`

Bottom third:
- Big orange rounded rectangle button graphic (not clickable, just an image element):
  - `Start free trial →`
- Below button, small slate-grey text: `14-day trial · no card · first 50 lock in $79/mo forever`

### Alternative: 15-sec animated version (harder to make, better performance)

Same composition, but with these animations:
- 0-2s: `❌ 3 hrs typing` types in with strikethrough
- 2-4s: `✅ 30 min reviewing` types in below, animates larger
- 4-8s: Divider draws in left-to-right, then `Photo + voice → AI draft → approve.` fades in one word at a time
- 8-11s: Small text appears
- 11-15s: Button pulses gently, hold on end frame

Made in After Effects, Rive, or Canva Pro's video timeline.

### Meta ad copy paired with this creative

- **Primary text:** "The math for a 20-inspection-a-month inspector: 50 hours a month back. Try Lookover free →"
- **Headline:** `You: 3 hrs. Lookover: 30 min.`
- **CTA:** `Sign Up`
- **URL:** `https://www.uselookover.com/signup?utm_source=meta&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=math-static&utm_content=v1`

## Which to shoot first?

**Shoot in this order — each one 5x cheaper to test than the previous:**

1. **Script 3 (the math static)** — 30 minutes in Figma. Ship today. Serves as the baseline for the ad set.
2. **Script 1 (founder explainer)** — 30 min shooting + 30 min editing + 30 min captioning. Ship tomorrow. This is the one that'll win.
3. **Script 2 (silent demo)** — 2-3 hours because you need to screen-record all the product moments cleanly. Ship this week. Runs as a differentiator against the founder video.

Once the ad set has all three running, Meta's algorithm picks the winner within 3-7 days at $30/day. Kill losers, feed the winner more budget per the rules in `campaign-agent-prompt.md`.

## Do-not-do reminders (from OFFER-POLICY.md)

- Never "free for life" in ad copy. That's for earned design partners only.
- Never mention cost estimates or repair pricing. US inspectors are legally barred.
- Never "AI-powered" in visible text. Use "drafts / writes / suggests".
- Never buyer-facing framing. This is inspector-audience media.
- Never use exclamation points. Anywhere.
