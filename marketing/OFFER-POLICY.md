## The three cohorts

| Cohort | Cap | Offer | Earns it by |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Design partners** | **10 total** | Free lifetime account | Using it on real client work + honest feedback + a **short video review** + permission to quote. Free-for-life is *earned*, not given. |
| **Founding members** | First 50 paying | **$79/mo, locked for life** (vs $129 standard) | Just paying. 14-day free trial first, no card to start. |
| **Standard** | Everyone after | $129/mo | 14-day trial → paid. |

## Why

The point of the beta is to **learn who will pay**. Giving free-for-life to everyone destroys that signal and creates a permanent $0 cohort. So free-for-life is reserved for a capped group whose **video testimonial** is worth more than the MRR (it fuels the InterNACHI / YouTuber / agent-referral stages). Everyone else is a real paying funnel visible in `/admin`.

This matches `STRATEGY.md` line 104 ($79 founding for first 50) and line 173 (free lifetime *in exchange for a review*).

## Messaging rules by channel

- **Reviewer / YouTuber / forum outreach:** "free lifetime account **in exchange for an honest video review**" — correct, this is the earned design-partner offer. Keep.
- **Cold paid ads:** Do **NOT** dangle free-for-life — it attracts freeloaders and burns design-partner intent on randoms. Lead with the product value + "$79/mo founding price for the first 50 · 14-day free trial · no card." Scarcity on the *founding price*, not on free.
- **In-product / auto-feedback email:** Offer the free-for-life design-partner slot **tied to the video**, framed as limited ("first handful"). Never "free either way."
- **Onboarding call:** Same — founding $79 is the default; free-for-life is the earned design-partner exception.

## What standard signups should hear

"14-day free trial, no credit card. After that, $79/mo locked in as a founding member if you're one of the first 50 — that price never goes up, even when the standard price does."
